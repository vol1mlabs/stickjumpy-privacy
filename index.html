StickJUMPY Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

StickJUMPY ("the game") is a mobile game published by VOL1M LABS ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

1. Information we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or location. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics.

  • Anonymous player ID. The first time you play online, the game creates an anonymous player ID through Unity Authentication. It is not linked to your real identity.
  • Player name and avatar. You choose a display name and avatar, or the game gives you a random name. Both are shown to other players on the leaderboard. Please do not use your real name or personal information as your display name.
  • Scores. Your weekly best score is sent to Unity Leaderboards together with your player ID, display name and avatar so the ranking can be shown.
  • Technical data. Like any online service, Unity's services may process technical data such as your IP address and device type to deliver the service and keep it secure.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, unlocked characters and worlds, best score, settings) is saved only on your device. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this progress is deleted.

3. How we use information

We use the information only to run the game: to show the weekly leaderboard, keep your score under your anonymous ID and keep the service working. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Third-party service

Online features are provided by Unity Technologies (Unity Authentication and Unity Leaderboards). Their handling of data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy. Data may be processed on servers outside your country.

5. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience but is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a display name, contact us and we will remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard is active and are replaced as new weeks begin. To request deletion of your online data (anonymous ID, display name, avatar and scores), email us at the address below and tell us your display name. Uninstalling the game removes the local data on your device.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
