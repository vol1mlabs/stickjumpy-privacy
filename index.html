Privacy Policy

StickJUMPY by VOL1M LABS
Last updated: October 14, 2026

StickJUMPY ("the game") is a mobile game published by VOL1M LABS ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why it is used and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • You do not need to create an account, and we do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or location.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party analytics.
  • Your progress stays only on your device.
  • Only the online weekly leaderboard sends a small amount of data to our service provider, Unity.

2. Data stored only on your device

Your progress (coins, unlocked characters and worlds, best score and settings) is saved only on your device. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this progress is deleted.

3. Data used for the online leaderboard

When you play online, the game uses Unity Authentication and Unity Leaderboards, provided by Unity Technologies. The following is processed:

DataPurposeVisible to other players
Anonymous player ID (created the first time you play online, not linked to your real identity)Keep your score under your own entryNo
Display name (chosen by you, or a random name given by the game)Show you on the leaderboardYes
AvatarShow you on the leaderboardYes
Your weekly best scoreRank players on the leaderboardYes

Like any online service, Unity's services may also process technical data such as your IP address and device type to deliver the service and keep it secure. Please do not use your real name or personal information as your display name.

4. How we use information

We use the information only to run the game: to show the weekly leaderboard, keep your score under your anonymous ID and keep the service working. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Third-party service

Online features are provided by Unity Technologies. Their handling of data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy. Data may be processed on servers outside your country.

6. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience but is not designed to collect personal information from children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a display name, contact us and we will remove it.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard is active and are replaced as new weeks begin.
  • To request deletion of your online data (anonymous ID, display name, avatar and scores), email us at vol1mlabshelp@gmail.com and tell us your display name.
  • Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
